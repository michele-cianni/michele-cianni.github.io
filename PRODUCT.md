# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Freelance/SME clients in Italy and Germany (industrial/agricultural machinery sector) evaluating Michele as a developer for their business site. They land here mid-evaluation, comparing developers/agencies, and want proof via real outcomes — not a tech-stack list. Job to be done: decide "can this person build something that brings my business customers."

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that doubles as a client-acquisition tool, framed around real outcomes rather than a tech-stack list. Showcases the two real freelance projects (G&M Escavatori, Ledershow) as case studies narrated problem → built → result, not as technology lists. Bilingual IT (default) / EN via native Astro i18n, for SEO reach in both markets. Success looks like a prospective SME client reading a case study and reaching out.

## Brand Personality

Warm, artisanal, trustworthy — a craftsperson's voice, not a hype machine. First-person, direct, results-focused. Honest about real metrics only; no invented numbers.

## Anti-references

The "AI-slop dev portfolio" template this redesign explicitly replaces: dark `#0A0A0F` background with neon purple `#6C63FF` accent, dot-grid + radial glow repeated in every section, fake terminal window UI (`developer.ts`, `// new_message.md`), decorative mono eyebrows (`// 01 — about.me`), glassmorphism / `backdrop-blur` on every card, pulsing "available for opportunities" green dot, blinking cursor, scroll-hint shimmer, oversized Bebas Neue display type, fake browser-mockup project cards.

## Design Principles

- Carta, non terminale: the paper/craftsperson metaphor replaces the dev-tool/terminal metaphor everywhere.
- Risultati reali, non promesse: only true outcomes get written down; honest qualitative copy stands in where no real metric exists yet.
- Il cliente è protagonista: case studies are framed around the client's problem and outcome; tech stack is a caption, not the headline.
- Massimo due accenti: terracotta + oxblood carry the entire identity; no third competing color.
- Bilingue per davvero: IT/EN are real routed pages (Astro i18n) for SEO, not a client-side string-swap toggle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast required on the paper palette (ink-on-paper, accent-on-paper). `prefers-reduced-motion` respected everywhere — carry forward the pattern already implemented for the current scroll-reveal.
